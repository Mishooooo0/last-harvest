## How to play and test

- **Play:** start the `last-harvest` preview (port 4173), or run `node serve.mjs` in this folder and open http://localhost:4173
- **Test:** `node engine-test.mjs` (32 tests, about 1 second)
- **Files:**
  - `dist/engine.js` has all the game rules, with no drawing.
  - `dist/renderer.js` draws everything.
  - `dist/main.js` holds controls, menus, sound, the journal and the wheel.
  - `dist/index.html` and `dist/style.css` are the page.
  - There is no build step: `dist/` *is* the source.

## Update 8 — Squad tactics

- Shift-click the ground or a task to queue orders. The queue executes in order, ending in a hold when finished.
- Ctrl+1–4 saves a control group; Alt+1–4 recalls it. Existing number keys still select individual people.
- Squad Orders offers a two-point patrol, Guard here, Retreat inside, and Send to clinic. Back to work cancels orders and patrols.
- Fire discipline: normal attacks, Hold fire, or Conserve shells. Conserving slows shooting and reserves the final five shells for nearby threats.
- Selected people show route and destination marks; their skills, traits, morale and queue length appear in the management panel.

## Update 9 — Farming, gates, weather and treatment

- Barn upgrades add a hay loft, extra cattle capacity, stronger cattle and reinforced doors. Harvests collect hay while the barn is upgraded; stored hay restores cattle health at breakfast.
- Gates cost 5 scrap and require construction. Closed gates block zombies; open gates are real entrances. The manager can open or close all gates together. People still pass through defenses.
- Each of the 15 plots has an independent next-crop choice. Potatoes grow quickly and supply food; trade corn gives more scrap; pumpkins grow slowly and give a large food harvest. The original seed-stock upgrades remain available.
- Morning weather can be clear, rain, dry or fog. Rain fills built water tanks; dry weather consumes surplus irrigation water and slows growth more when reserves are low; fog reduces firearm visibility/range. Rain, fog and dry light also appear on the map.
- A three-level Clinic and a Medic job. Medics treat nearby patients over time; use Send to clinic on the wounded. Comfortable clinic beds also improve breakfast recovery and morale.

## Update 10 — New night threats

- Crawlers appear from day 2: they pass beneath ordinary fences, but spikes and traps hurt them.
- Spitters appear from day 2: they fire ranged acid attacks at exposed people. Shotguns outrange them in clear weather.
- Screamers appear from day 3: close to the farmhouse they call two reinforcements once. Stop them before they get close.
- Every fifth night is a larger siege with one Colossus, a large and tough brute. New enemies have distinct appearances and explanatory hover text.
- Returning scouts and expeditions provide horde forecasts. Night notices warn about the expected threats.

## Update 11 — Expeditions

- Four map destinations: abandoned farm, gas station, roadside clinic and silent town. People can discover places on foot, and scouts report discoveries.
- Send an individually chosen team by daylight, packing one food and one water per person. The menu shows travel time and base injury risk; armed escorts, scouting skills and sharp eyes reduce risk.
- Teams travel out, pause for a real choice at the destination, then return: rescue a survivor, search deeper for 60% more loot at greater injury risk, or return with normal supplies.
- Loot includes scrap, seeds, food, water, gasoline, medicine and radio components. Storage limits still apply. Rescues respect the eight-person farmhouse capacity; expedition injuries are recorded by the journal.
- Recovered expedition blueprints unlock two firearm upgrades. Completed missions are recorded, and unfinished missions survive save/load. One expedition may be active at a time.

## Update 12 — People and the radio campaign

- People learn farming, building, scouting, combat and medicine through work. Experience gives up to five skill levels with relevant work, damage, treatment and expedition benefits.
- Each person has a useful trait and a weakness: craftsperson/slow walker, green thumb/fragile, sharp-eyed/big appetite, or steady nerves/heavy sleeper. These affect work, movement, health, provisions, expedition risk or behavior in darkness.
- Morale improves with meals, successful expeditions and comfortable buildings, and falls with hunger, injuries and deaths. It affects work and treatment efficiency.
- A Radio campaign option adds a longer goal. Build the receiver, recover an aerial component from the gas station or town, order and construct the repaired aerial, then survive through the tenth night and its final siege.
- The campaign continues past night 3, has its own progress display and victory message, and does not overwrite the endless-mode record.
